## Why V3

V2 showed five structural problems in real mobile review:
1. section changes still read too much like slide cuts;
2. Location used a weak overlay/card structure;
3. Agenda lost premium value because text sat inside a basic rectangular panel;
4. Practical became long, flat and editorially weak;
5. RSVP/Closing did not resolve as one complete final act and left a dead-feeling tail.

## V3 structural corrections

### Global flow
- no explicit slide-transition device;
- soft seam gradients between visual worlds;
- fewer full-height hard section boundaries;
- shared ivory/paper/light system to preserve continuity.

### Location
- no floating information card;
- venue image is full-bleed;
- name/time/address/actions live directly inside the photographic scene;
- bottom gradient supports legibility without creating a separate box.

### Agenda
- no text card;
- A02 moving botanical field is visible and remains part of the design;
- timeline content is native over the scene;
- only line/dots and typography structure the information.

### Practical
- reduced copy length;
- compressed into a 2×2 editorial grid on standard mobile widths;
- no endless vertical list;
- botanical motion remains visible at the side;
- content hierarchy becomes number → title → one-line explanation → action.

### Final act
- RSVP + closing copy are permanently one section;
- one botanical moving background;
- CTA, names, date and closing sentence all visible in the same final composition;
- no post-close scroll runway or empty final screen.

### Cover
- A01 retained;
- name reveal pacing simplified into three stages;
- no blur-mask behavior.

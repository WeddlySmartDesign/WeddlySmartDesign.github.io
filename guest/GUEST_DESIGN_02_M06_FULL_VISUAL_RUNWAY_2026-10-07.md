## Photo-integration system under test

### Story
- no generic card/frame;
- edge-to-edge photo field;
- dark cinematic grading;
- hard optical glass/refraction material over the image;
- refracted champagne edge to link photo and NOCTURNE material language.

### Location
- venue photograph remains the dominant scene;
- NOCTURNE overlays only provide tonal continuity and legibility;
- no fake venue architecture generated.

### Gallery
- full photographic slides;
- no film-strip/contact-sheet/Polaroid motif;
- no arch/circle/frame;
- each photo receives the same optical material language as a live overlay;
- natural horizontal swipe remains the interaction.

### Closing
- couple photo fades directly into the black final act;
- no framed-photo UI;
- names/date remain native text.

## Important prototype boundary

This is NOT:
- final typography;
- final fixed copy;
- final motion timing;
- scalability certification;
- template registry admission;
- publication.

The purpose is one decision:
**Does the complete NOCTURNE language make sense once real couple photos and the full invitation flow are visible?**

## Gate

Owner reviews the whole prototype once, preferably as one continuous mobile scroll.

If the full arc still feels unclear, too corporate/luxury-brand-like, or photography does not belong naturally:
- do not keep polishing;
- reopen/reject the NOCTURNE art direction before building the production template.

If the full arc works:
- seal visual direction;
- move to complete scalable template architecture and configuration-matrix build.

## Global principle

The invitation must NOT repeat the same floral hero treatment in every section.

The visual system uses three intensity levels:

### HIGH — cinematic event
Used only where a strong premium moment is justified.
- Opening / Cover
- Closing

### MEDIUM — living atmosphere
Used where content and imagery must remain dominant.
- Story
- Location
- Gallery transitions

### LOW — quiet support
Used where reading/function must dominate.
- Agenda
- Practical
- RSVP

Every section remains visually coherent through:
- daylight;
- petal translucency;
- botanical depth;
- warm ivory / blush / champagne / soft green;
- realistic natural movement;
- shared typography;
- native content;
- controlled foreground botanical movement.

No section is allowed to become a static floral wallpaper.

## Photo rule

Photography is never:
- blurred for integration;
- darkened globally;
- tinted until identity is lost;
- placed in arch/circle/frame.

Photography remains:
- sharp;
- naturally colored;
- emotionally dominant;
- protected from botanical occlusion around faces.

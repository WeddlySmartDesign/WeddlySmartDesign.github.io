## Product
GUEST sells digital wedding invitations personalized by WeddlySmartDesign, with RSVP and a full guest-management engine behind them.

Commercial entry point:
1. Couple chooses Essential or Signature.
2. WeddlySmartDesign personalizes it.
3. Delivery target: 24–48 h.
4. Couple sends invitation.
5. RSVP updates GUEST automatically.
6. Couple manages guests, +1, menus/allergies, transport, accommodation, custom questions, groups/subgroups, tables, lists and extra events.

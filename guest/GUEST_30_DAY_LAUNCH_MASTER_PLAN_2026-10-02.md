## Validated production stack
- Visual art direction: Recraft / premium generative visual assets.
- Premium motion: image-to-video on the approved premium still.
- Motion quality gate: preserve original composition and art; animate material/light/object motion subtly; no morphing/warping.
- Invitation implementation: native web layer for typography, copy, CTA, countdown, agenda, story, gallery navigation and GUEST connection.
- RSVP: CTA into existing GUEST RSVP. No duplicate inline RSVP.

## Rejected production methods
- HTML/CSS motion of one flattened raster.
- Fake depth by clipping/copying one raster into moving zones.
- Manual DOM/SVG reconstruction of rich generative art.
- Overloaded art direction combining too many luxury signals at once.

## Catalog architecture — six genuinely different launch lines
### 01 — VEIL LIGHT
Dominant language: translucent veil + warm light.
No pearls as a primary cue. Florals minimal or absent.
Broad, romantic, contemporary, high-end.
Motion: slow veil movement, light breathing, restrained camera push.
This is the FIRST real design to build.

### 02 — MIDNIGHT GARDEN
Dominant language: nocturnal garden + points of warm light.
Deep greens / black plum / ivory.
Motion: light shimmer, subtle leaves/petals, slow depth shift.
No silk/veil dependency.

### 03 — MEDITERRANEAN HOUR
Dominant language: Mediterranean architecture + sunset/blue-hour atmosphere.
Stone, water, cypress/olive, soft terracotta.
Motion: light, foliage, water/air, very slow camera movement.
Venue personalization can be especially strong here.

### 04 — INK BLOOM
Dominant language: hand-painted ink / wash botanical artwork.
Off-white paper, black/sepia ink, one restrained accent color.
Motion: controlled bloom/draw/bleed effect, not full cinematic scene motion.
More artistic and minimal than romantic.

### 05 — PORCELAIN
Dominant language: refined ceramic / painted ornament / cobalt or sage detail.
White/ivory base, strong decorative identity but generous negative space.
Motion: subtle painted element movement / light / reveal.
No fabric-led visual language.

### 06 — NOIR MONOGRAM
Dominant language: dark formal invitation + monogram / light / restrained ornamental detail.
Black, ivory, metallic-warm accents.
Motion: light reveal, monogram build, subtle depth.
Modern/formal counterpoint to the softer catalog lines.

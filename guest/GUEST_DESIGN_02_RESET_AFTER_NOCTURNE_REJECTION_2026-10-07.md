# GUEST — DESIGN 02 RESET AFTER NOCTURNE REJECTION

Date: 2026-10-07
Branch: guest-independent
Status: SEALED RESET CONTRACT

## Decision
NOCTURNE is rejected permanently.

## Non-negotiable lessons

### 1. Premium must be visible without explanation
If the customer needs to be told why a design is technically sophisticated, it is not premium enough.

### 2. Recraft must create product value
Recraft is to be used for realistic, high-end motion/design assets that ordinary Canva users cannot plausibly recreate.

Do not spend Recraft credits on:
- generic light sweeps;
- atmospheric dark backgrounds;
- effects that can be replaced by CSS fades/pans;
- footage whose only function is decorative ambience.

### 3. Photography must gain value
Couple photography must remain sharp, emotionally legible and important.

Never solve photo integration by:
- excessive darkening;
- blur;
- hiding most of the image;
- tinting every photo until it loses its character.

The template must make ordinary real couple photography look more considered and premium than the source, while preserving the photo itself.

### 4. No Canva-equivalent section
Any major section that can be recreated with:
- a background;
- serif typography;
- a photo;
- a line;
- fade/reveal/scroll animation;
is rejected before owner review.

### 5. Motion must be a designed event
At least one reusable realistic motion system must be structurally central to the invitation and visibly hard to reproduce manually.

### 6. Catalog distance
Design 02 must be obviously different from:
- VEIL LIGHT: ivory/beige, veil/fabric, quiet luxury, soft romance;
- rejected NOCTURNE: dark ink, architectural/optical luxury, restrained black-tie editorial.

### 7. Review economy
Do not bring the owner incremental versions.
Internally eliminate weak concepts and show only:
- one defensible visual territory;
- one serious motion gate;
- then one integrated full-arc review.

## Market boundary observed
Generic floral/botanical website invitations are already commoditised, including hand-designed premium competitors and DIY Canva products.
Therefore Design 02 must not be merely "floral", "botanical", "garden", or "colourful".

The next territory must derive its defensibility from material + realistic motion + integration, not motif alone.

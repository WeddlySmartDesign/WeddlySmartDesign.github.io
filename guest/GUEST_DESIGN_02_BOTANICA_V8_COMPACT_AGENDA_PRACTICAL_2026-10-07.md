Date: 2026-10-07
Branch: guest-independent
Status: MOBILE REVIEW PENDING

## Trigger
Real Android review of V7 showed:
- Agenda and Practical were too long;
- neither fit in one mobile screen;
- Practical labels were too close together;
- arrow glyphs were ambiguous as interaction affordances.

## V8 correction
### Agenda
- exactly one mobile viewport;
- five moments only;
- time + label, no secondary copy;
- stable vertical composition;
- no cards;
- no scroll-state animation;
- A02 remains visible as living background.

### Practical
- exactly one mobile viewport;
- 2×2 editorial matrix without card shadows/background blocks;
- concise labels;
- explicit "Abrir" affordance replaces ambiguous arrows;
- detailed information remains in the existing bottom sheet on tap;
- spacing increased to prevent text collisions.

## Global rule added
For this Design 02 family:
- Agenda must fit in one mobile screen.
- Practical must fit in one mobile screen.
- Functional detail belongs behind interaction, not exposed as long primary-page copy.
- Interaction affordances must be explicit on mobile.

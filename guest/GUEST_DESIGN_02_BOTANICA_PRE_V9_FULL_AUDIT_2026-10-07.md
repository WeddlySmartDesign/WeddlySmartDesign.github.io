## Why this audit exists

V1–V8 proved that fixing Agenda/Practical only at CSS/layout level creates repeated variants of the same problem.

The full invitation has now been reviewed against:
- the canonical GUEST master;
- catalog template/plugin contracts;
- the NOCTURNE rejection lessons;
- BOTÁNICA visual-system rules;
- real Android recordings through V8.

V9 must be a deliberate global convergence pass, not another local layout experiment.

---

## 1. GLOBAL VERDICT

BOTÁNICA CINEMÁTICA remains a valid Design 02 direction.

What is genuinely strong:
- wedding relevance is immediate;
- A01 opening motion is premium and meaningful;
- real photography can stay sharp/natural;
- floral translucency + daylight + depth create a coherent visual world;
- Cover → Countdown → Story is the strongest continuous run built so far.

What is not yet commercially acceptable:
- the middle functional content still falls back to generic web/Canva visual grammar;
- typography in prototypes is placeholder/system-like and contributes to the basic look;
- one quiet support motion (A02) has been forced to solve too many different sections;
- Agenda and Practical have repeatedly been treated as UI/content-layout problems instead of art-direction problems;
- final act is structurally correct as RSVP+Closing together, but still uses a support asset rather than a dedicated emotional closing asset.

Conclusion:
DO NOT solve V9 by another timeline/grid/menu variation.

### Agenda — REDESIGN FROM ART DIRECTION
All V2–V8 agenda solutions are rejected:
- cards;
- standard timeline;
- zig-zag;
- scroll-state kinetic stack;
- compact timeline.

Reason:
even when functional, they remain Canva/web-equivalent.

V9 Agenda must use a NEW PURPOSE-BUILT BOTANICAL MOTION ASSET.

Working asset:
**A05 — AGENDA BOTANICAL SPINE**

Art direction:
- same daylight/petal/translucency world;
- one recognisable real stem/branch travels through frame;
- 5 natural visual anchor areas / pauses created by the botanical composition;
- subtle physically believable living motion;
- no literal UI line/timeline generated in HTML;
- native times + short moment names align to the scene;
- supports deterministic 1–5 moments;
- entire Agenda fits one mobile screen;
- no secondary explanatory sentences in the main view.

Premium source:
the botanical spine itself + living depth, not a CSS line.

### Practical — REMOVE AS “FEATURE MENU”
Reject:
- grids;
- cards;
- four floating items;
- arrows;
- ABRIR / VER / MÁS INFORMACIÓN / product-interface language;
- visibly tiny copy.

V9 Practical becomes a **COUPLE-WRITTEN NOTE**, not a menu.

Main-screen principle:
one short natural paragraph, large enough to read comfortably, with only the relevant enabled details embedded as tappable phrases.

Example voice:
“Para que no tengáis que preguntarnos uno por uno: habrá autobuses de vuelta, hemos reservado algunas habitaciones cerca y podéis dejarnos vuestra canción aquí.”

Gift example if enabled:
“Y si aun así os apetece tener un detalle, os dejamos esto aquí.”

Interaction:
- tappable phrase itself;
- no visible command verb;
- detail opens in existing elegant sheet/panel;
- disabled modules disappear and sentence recomposes deterministically;
- 0–4 module states require certified copy rules.

Visual:
- A02 may remain subtly alive;
- the premium design is the large editorial note + living botanical field;
- no boxes.

### Gallery — KEEP / LIGHT POLISH

### RSVP + Closing — KEEP AS ONE ACT / UPGRADE ASSET

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY — MUST BE SOLVED BEFORE COMMERCIAL V9

Prototype Georgia/Arial styling is not acceptable as final premium typography.

V9 must introduce a real BOTÁNICA typography system:
- one refined display serif with personality but high mobile legibility;
- one human, quiet sans-serif for functional/support copy;
- one restrained italic/secondary treatment only where needed.

Requirements:
- clearly different from VEIL LIGHT typography;
- deterministic short/long-name sizes;
- Spanish accents render perfectly;
- readable at 360 px Android width;
- no microcopy below certified minimum size.

Do not rely on typography to compensate for weak art direction, but do not evaluate final premium quality using placeholder system fonts.

---

## 4. COPY RULE — HUMAN, NOT PRODUCT UI

Every visible sentence must plausibly sound like a couple writing to their guests.

Reject visible UI language where a human phrase can do the job:
- Abrir
- Ver…
- Más información
- Consultar
- Gestionar
- Acceder

Functional exceptions only when universally natural:
- Confirmar asistencia
- Cómo llegar

Preferred:
the phrase itself is the interactive target.

Copy is sample/content, not template-fixed personality.
The common config must be able to supply couple-specific text while the template handles length deterministically.

## 6. TRANSITIONS

No hard slide/page-cut feeling.

Use:
- gradient overlap;
- botanical continuity;
- photo-to-ivory bleed;
- background movement continuing under section boundary where relevant.

Do not use:
- wipe effects;
- full-screen flower occlusion;
- artificial transition videos;
- generic fade-to-white between every block.

## 10. V9 SUCCESS CRITERIA

V9 fails before owner review if:
- Agenda can be reproduced by swapping in a Canva timeline/list template;
- Practical reads as SaaS/product navigation;
- any major section uses a static wallpaper feel;
- text becomes tiny to make content fit;
- photography is degraded to fit the design;
- final visual authority is materially below the Cover;
- a supported state needs manual layout repair.

V9 passes visual direction only when the invitation feels intentionally designed from Cover through final RSVP/Closing, with quiet sections that remain premium rather than merely functional.

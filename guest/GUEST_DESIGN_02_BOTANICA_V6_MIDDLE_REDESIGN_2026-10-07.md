## Why V6 exists

V5 was rejected because Agenda and Practical were still the same basic architecture in a different style:
- Agenda remained a visible list/timeline.
- Practical remained a visible list/menu.

The problem was conceptual, not cosmetic.

## V6 structural changes

### Agenda
Agenda is no longer displayed as a complete list.

It is now a single continuous cinematic scene:
- A02 remains the moving botanical environment.
- The viewport stays visually continuous while scrolling.
- One agenda moment occupies the scene at a time.
- Large native time + editorial title + short natural copy.
- Five moments crossfade/shift inside the same scene.
- No cards.
- No timeline table.
- No full list visible at once.

Goal:
make Agenda feel like a designed passage through the day rather than a website component.

### Practical
Practical is no longer a standalone long section.

It is now a compact epilogue inside the same botanical world:
- title;
- one short explanatory sentence;
- four typographic access points only:
  Transporte / Alojamiento / La música / Un detalle.
- full detail remains available on tap in the existing bottom sheet.

Goal:
preserve function without allowing utility information to dominate the invitation.

### Continuity
Agenda + Practical remain one continuous A02 visual world.
The handoff into Gallery is softened.

## Preserved unchanged

- Story
- Location
- Gallery
- Unified RSVP + closing act

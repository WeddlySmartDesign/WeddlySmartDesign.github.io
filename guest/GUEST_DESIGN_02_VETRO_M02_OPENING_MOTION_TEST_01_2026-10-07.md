## What passes
- realistic blown-glass material remains premium;
- daylight/refraction language is clearly distinct from VEIL LIGHT and rejected NOCTURNE;
- motion has materially more premium value than ordinary Canva-style animation;
- glass remains visually compatible with native wedding typography;
- concept is strong enough to justify one focused corrective motion pass.

## What fails
- movement reads partly as AI morphing rather than designed physical articulation;
- glass forms change shape too much instead of behaving like stable sculptural pieces;
- central negative space does not open decisively enough;
- there is insufficient sense of separate foreground/midground elements rotating or moving in real 3D;
- the opening event lacks a clear beginning → transformation → resting composition.

## Required correction
Next pass must:
- preserve each glass form's geometry;
- move pieces as rigid sculptural elements;
- use slow rotation / separation / slight translation;
- open the central negative space intentionally;
- preserve optical refraction and caustics as consequences of physical motion;
- end in a stable typography-ready composition;
- use exact 9:16.

Do not build the invitation yet.

This is unacceptable for the final premium opening because the design promise is realistic physical glass motion.

## Decision
Stop Seedance iteration.

VETRO has passed the concept-value gate strongly enough to justify exactly one higher-fidelity motion generation with a physics/consistency-focused model.

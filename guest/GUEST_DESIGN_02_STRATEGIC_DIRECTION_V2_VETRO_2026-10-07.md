# GUEST — DESIGN 02 STRATEGIC DIRECTION V2 — VETRO

Date: 2026-10-07
Branch: guest-independent
Status: PROPOSED / MOTION GATE REQUIRED
Working name: VETRO

## Core idea
A bright, luminous wedding invitation built around realistic sculptural blown glass in motion.

Not stained glass.
Not flat florals.
Not botanical illustration.
Not a dark luxury website.

The premium device is a reusable three-dimensional glass sculpture:
- translucent petals / organic abstract forms;
- real refraction;
- caustic sunlight;
- layered depth;
- physically believable movement;
- foreground/background occlusion.

Recraft is used to create realistic reusable motion assets that cannot be plausibly reproduced with normal Canva animation.

## Catalog distance

### Versus VEIL LIGHT
- bright colour instead of ivory quiet-luxury neutrality;
- hard translucent glass instead of soft veil/fabric;
- sunlight/refraction instead of textile movement;
- sculptural motion instead of calm material drift;
- photography remains crisp and vivid.

### Versus rejected NOCTURNE
- daylight rather than darkness;
- colour rather than black;
- organic sculpture rather than architecture;
- physical 3D motion rather than CSS optical effects;
- photographs gain brightness and depth rather than being subdued.

## Visual world
- luminous ivory / daylight base;
- clear glass;
- blush / apricot / celadon / champagne;
- optional controlled cobalt or wine micro-accent;
- realistic caustics and refracted colour;
- no metallic gold dependence;
- no flat pastel Canva palette.

## Opening
A macro cinematic view of one sculptural glass object.

At rest, several translucent organic glass planes overlap.
During the opening:
- glass forms slowly rotate / unfold in real 3D;
- sunlight travels through the material;
- caustics move across the scene;
- the opening creates a clean negative-space area;
- native names/date appear inside that revealed space.

The movement itself is the premium event.
No envelope.
No curtain.
No flat flower animation.
No generic fade.

## Photography rule
Photography must become more valuable, never less.

Photos remain:
- sharp;
- naturally coloured;
- emotionally readable;
- large/full-bleed where appropriate.

NO blur as integration technique.
NO heavy universal darkening.
NO tint that destroys the source.

Integration:
- reusable glass sculpture passes in foreground;
- refracted light/caustics can move over a small edge region;
- glass can create depth/occlusion around, never over, important faces;
- source photo remains the visual hero.

## Story
Large sharp real couple photograph.
A glass element moves across the foreground and reveals/bridges to short story copy.
Text occupies clean daylight/ivory space, not a generic card.

No-photo state uses the sculptural glass scene, not an empty panel.

## Location
Real venue photo remains clear and recognizable.
One glass/refraction edge creates continuity.
Information is native and readable.
No fake generated venue.

## Agenda
Bright sculptural composition with 1–5 moments.
A moving caustic/refraction path progresses through the schedule.
No repeated cards/icons.

## Practical
Compact information integrated into a sculptural daylight scene.
The glass object creates spatial divisions rather than CSS boxes.
0–4 states recomposed deterministically.

## Gallery
Photos remain full, sharp and high-value.
Transitions use a realistic glass element passing close to camera / refracting an edge before the next photo.
No contact sheet, Polaroid, carousel-card aesthetic.

## RSVP
Bright calm final act.
One sculptural glass form creates the interaction focus.
Native CTA.
No dark generic footer.

## Closing
A related glass sculpture resolves / settles into a quieter composition.
Names/date remain native.
Closing movement is a real material event, not a fade.

## Recraft production philosophy
Recraft assets should be:
- realistic moving material/objects;
- reusable;
- independent of couple names/photos;
- visually valuable even before CSS;
- structurally difficult to recreate in Canva.

Expected reusable motion asset family:
1. opening glass sculpture unfold;
2. one transition / foreground glass pass;
3. closing resolve.

Still assets only where necessary for continuity/reduced motion.

## Scalability
No Recraft generation per couple.

Couple-specific:
- native names/data;
- sharp photos;
- CROP/FULL + focal point;
- bounded certified palette/typography presets.

Template-specific:
- all glass video/assets;
- transitions;
- caustics;
- geometry;
- motion timings.

## Anti-Canva gate
Reject if the premium perception can survive after replacing the glass motion with:
- stock PNG flowers;
- fade;
- pan;
- simple video background;
- Canva element animation.

The invitation must depend visibly on:
- realistic refraction;
- material depth;
- physical 3D motion;
- foreground occlusion;
- moving caustics;
- integration with native content/photos.

## First gate
Do not build the invitation.

Create only:
- one opening source frame;
- one 5–6 second realistic opening motion;
- native names/date mock overlay after motion selection.

If that opening does not visibly exceed Canva-level work and show a clear path for sharp photography, reject VETRO before any further build.
